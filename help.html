Get involved

There are many ways to make a difference.

Peace & Unity grows through people who are willing to give their time, skills, resources, encouragement, and compassion. Whether you volunteer, partner with us, give, or simply spread the word, your support can help someone feel seen and supported.

🤝

Volunteer

Give your time, skills, and energy to support community activities, outreach, visits, practical assistance, and people who may need a helping hand.

📣

Spread the Word

Tell your friends, family, colleagues, and community about Peace & Unity. Sharing our message can help connect people who need support with people who are willing to help.

💛

Give With Purpose

Your contribution can help provide practical support to vulnerable families, children, and people facing difficult circumstances.

🏢

Partner With Us

Organisations and businesses can contribute resources, skills, sponsorship, expertise, or other forms of practical support.

💡

Share Your Skills

Your professional knowledge or personal skills may be valuable. We welcome people who want to contribute what they know and can do.

🌱

Bring an Idea

Have an idea that could help a family, child, or community? We would love to hear it and explore how we can work together.

Want to get involved?

Contact Peace & Unity and tell us how you would like to help. Every act of kindness matters.
