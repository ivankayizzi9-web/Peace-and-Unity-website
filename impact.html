Your support in action

Where your support can make a difference.

Peace & Unity is committed to responding to practical needs and standing alongside people and families who are facing difficult circumstances. As the initiative grows, we want to connect kindness with real needs and meaningful opportunities to help.

🍲

Food & Basic Needs

Support can help provide essential items to people and families facing difficult circumstances, including food and other everyday necessities.

📚

Children & Education

Help can support children whose families are struggling with school fees and other needs that can make education difficult to continue.

🏠

Vulnerable Families

Support can help families facing hardship with practical assistance and a connection to people who are willing and able to help.

❤️

Emergency Support

When urgent needs arise, contributions can help Peace & Unity respond with compassion and practical support where possible.

🤝

Community Support

We want to bring people together around local needs and encourage collective action that strengthens communities.

🌱

Hope & Opportunity

Our long-term aim is to create pathways to support, opportunity, and hope for people who may otherwise feel forgotten.

Our commitment

Peace & Unity will continue building practical ways to connect support with real needs. As the initiative grows, we will share more updates about the people, families, and community projects being supported.
