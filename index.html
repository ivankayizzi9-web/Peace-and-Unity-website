Growing peace, together. ✳ What we stand for Our Mission To bring hope, practical support, and opportunity closer to people and communities facing difficult circumstances. Peace & Unity was created from a belief that no one should feel forgotten when they are going through hardship. We want to connect compassion with practical action and encourage people to stand together for one another. Hope Encouraging people through difficult seasons and reminding them that every person’s story matters. Support Connecting care and practical help with people and families who need it. Opportunity Supporting pathways that can help children, families, and communities move forward. My personal story Tears Behind Our Smiles By Ivan Kayizzi I was born into a life of struggle. My mother raised us as a single mother, doing everything she could to give us a better life. We grew up in a small house, where life was never easy. School was very far from home, and paying school fees was a constant struggle. Sometimes, we had no choice but to walk long distances to school barefoot. I watched my mother struggle every day. There were moments when I saw her crying silently, trying to hide her pain from us. She carried so much on her shoulders, yet she continued to fight for her children. Then, my sister received an opportunity to travel abroad to Saudi Arabia. As a family, we were very happy for her. We hoped that this opportunity would change her life and bring better days for all of us. She had only been there for about two months when we began noticing changes in her. The person we knew seemed different, and we could not understand what was happening to her. At the time, our family believed that she had been bewitched. Eventually, she was deported and returned home. We immediately took her to the hospital, hoping that she would receive the help she needed. We prayed and hoped that she would become herself again, but nothing seemed to change. For about five years, our family struggled with her condition. Those years were extremely difficult. We watched someone we loved go through a painful journey while we tried everything we could to support her. My mother continued to carry the burden, just as she had carried so many burdens throughout our childhood. The pain in our home was often hidden behind our smiles, but inside, we were hurting deeply. Thankfully, today my sister is doing well. Seeing her okay again is something I am deeply grateful for. After everything our family went through, her recovery reminded me that even during the darkest moments, we should never give up hope. Our story taught me that suffering is not always visible. Behind many smiles are tears, struggles, and stories that people may never know. And sometimes, the greatest strength is simply refusing to give up on the people we love. Why I Created Peace & Unity All these experiences shaped the person I am today. Growing up with little, watching my mother struggle silently, and witnessing the pain my family went through taught me that life is not easy for everyone. I understand what it means to lack basic necessities, to have dreams but no resources to achieve them, and to watch loved ones struggle without knowing where help will come from. That is why I created Peace & Unity. I created this platform for people who have very little or nothing at all. For children whose families cannot afford school fees, for struggling mothers and fathers, for vulnerable families, and for communities that are often forgotten. I want Peace & Unity to become a place where people can find hope, where those who are able to help can reach those who desperately need support, and where no one feels forgotten or abandoned. I may not be able to change the whole world alone, but I believe that together, through kindness, compassion, and collective action, we can change someone’s life. My dream is simple: to give others the hope, opportunities, and support that many people, including my own family, once desperately needed. Because I know what it means to struggle, I never want to ignore someone else’s struggle. “Peace & Unity — Together We Can Make a Difference.” — Ivan Kayizzi Every act of kindness matters See what your support can make possible.
