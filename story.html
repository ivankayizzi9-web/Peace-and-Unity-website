My personal story

Tears Behind Our Smiles

By Ivan Kayizzi

I was born into a life of struggle. My mother raised us as a single mother, doing everything she could to give us a better life.

We grew up in a small house, where life was never easy. School was very far from home, and paying school fees was a constant struggle. Sometimes, we had no choice but to walk long distances to school barefoot.

I watched my mother struggle every day. There were moments when I saw her crying silently, trying to hide her pain from us. She carried so much on her shoulders, yet she continued to fight for her children.

Then, my sister received an opportunity to travel abroad to Saudi Arabia. As a family, we were very happy for her. We hoped that this opportunity would change her life and bring better days for all of us.

She had only been there for about two months when we began noticing changes in her. The person we knew seemed different, and we could not understand what was happening to her.

At the time, our family believed that she had been bewitched. Eventually, she was deported and returned home. We immediately took her to the hospital, hoping that she would receive the help she needed.

Our family struggled with this situation for years. For almost five years, we continued looking for help and trying to understand what had happened. It was one of the most difficult periods our family had ever experienced.

Thankfully, my sister is now okay. But the experience changed the way I looked at life and at the struggles faced by other families.

I realised that behind many smiles there can be stories of pain, hardship, fear and hope that other people never see.

This is one of the reasons I created Peace & Unity: to help people who cannot afford basic necessities, to support vulnerable families and children, and to encourage communities to stand together.

Peace & Unity is built on a simple belief: when we choose compassion, practical support and unity, we can make someone's difficult journey a little lighter.
