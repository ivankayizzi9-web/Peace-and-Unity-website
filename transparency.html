Honesty • Responsibility • Trust

Every Contribution Deserves Accountability.

We believe that giving should be built on trust. Brighter Future Foundation is committed to responsible support, honest communication and keeping people informed about our journey.

Our Commitment to Donors

Every contribution represents someone's kindness and desire to make a difference. We respect that trust and are committed to handling support carefully and communicating openly.

Clear Purpose

We explain our mission and the needs we aim to address, including support for vulnerable children, education and families experiencing poverty.

Responsible Giving

Contributions are intended to support the foundation's stated charitable priorities. We aim to handle all support responsibly and keep appropriate records.

Honest Updates

We aim to share genuine updates about our activities, progress, challenges and future priorities as our work develops.

Respect and Privacy

We believe children, families, supporters and community members deserve dignity. Personal information and stories should be treated with care.

How We Approach Contributions

We want supporters to understand how contributions are received and how we intend to account for them.

1. Understand the purpose
We communicate the foundation's priorities and the needs that contributions are intended to support.
2. Confirm payment details
Supporters should verify the displayed recipient details before sending any contribution.
3. Keep payment records
Supporters should retain their transaction confirmations. The foundation aims to maintain appropriate records of contributions received.
4. Share progress
As activities develop, we aim to communicate how support is being used and what progress has been made.
Important organisational notice
Brighter Future Foundation is currently preparing for formal registration and the establishment of its organisational financial arrangements. At this stage, mobile-money contributions are received through the founder's personal account under the name Ivan Kayizzi. Please confirm the recipient name before making a payment. Organisational financial arrangements and further payment information will be communicated when established.

Our Promise of Open Communication

We do not want to make promises about results that have not yet happened. Instead, we aim to share accurate information as the foundation grows, including:

Activity Updates

Genuine information about programmes, community activities and progress.

Financial Information

Appropriate financial summaries and accountability information as records and organisational arrangements become established.

Questions About Giving?

We welcome questions from donors, volunteers and partners. Please contact us if you need clarification before contributing.
